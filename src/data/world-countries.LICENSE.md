# World boundaries

Natural Earth 1:110m Admin 0 countries, public domain.
Source: https://github.com/vasturiano/globe.gl/blob/master/example/datasets/ne_110m_admin_0_countries.geojson
Terms: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Projected once with d3-geo Equal Earth to a 960x480 view box; coordinates rounded to one decimal. Antarctica omitted. FR, NO and XK codes normalized. Bundled locally to avoid third-party requests and WebGL. Small territories absent at this scale remain in the country list. Boundaries are for general analytics, not legal determinations.
