# NecrotixLab 1.3.96

Booking addon 1.3.1 adds bilingual approved, pending, rescheduled and declined labels, preserves date-change history, and provides OWNER/ADMIN CSV exports for all, filtered or selected reservations. No payments are added.

Booking history cleanup retains future active appointments and removes records 30 days after meeting end or terminal status change. Related notification/reminder jobs cascade; independent work projects are detached and preserved. Apply the additive `20261003065000_booking_status_history` migration and schedule an authenticated daily retention cron (or retain the notification cron). External Cal.diy data, backups and downloaded exports have separate lifetimes.

Validation: TypeScript, ESLint, unit suite, client boundary audit and PostgreSQL migrations/foreign-key checks. SMTP delivery and real provider calendar configuration require deployment verification.
