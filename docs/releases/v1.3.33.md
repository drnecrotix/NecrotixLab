# v1.3.33

- Reader mode is available on Wiki publications: the main Wiki article, the dedicated FAQ, and Wiki article slugs. Works in a regular browser tab and in the installed app.
- The Wiki article index (`/wiki/articles`) stays a list and does not open the reader.
- Back-to-top is hidden on Wiki pages so it does not sit over the reader button.
- Wiki chrome (side TOC, infobox, index pills) is dropped while reader mode is on so the publication stays in the centre.
