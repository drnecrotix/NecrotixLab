# NecrotixLab 1.3.98

Booking 1.4.1 adds a four-step appointment flow: service, monthly calendar and available time, contact details, and review before submission. A persistent organizer summary shows duration, platform and timezone. Back navigation preserves entered details; a time conflict refreshes availability. Pending and confirmed outcomes have distinct confirmation text.

Admin Appointments now includes a monthly calendar with status indicators and a selected-day agenda linked to appointment details. Existing search/status filters apply to the calendar, and filtered CSV export follows the selected month using Europe/Sofia boundaries with daylight-saving handling. Calendar results are capped at 300 with a visible narrowing prompt.

The experience takes design cues from Easy!Appointments and Cal.diy, with original components and no new dependencies. Native scheduling, SMTP, approvals, calendar invitations, retention and exports remain available. No payment option is added.

Upgrade the compiled CMS and addon together. No database migration or external booking service is needed for this release. Validation is recorded in docs/booking-contact-calendar-qa.md.
