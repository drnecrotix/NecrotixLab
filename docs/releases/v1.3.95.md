# NecrotixLab 1.3.95 / Booking 1.3.0

- Client contact form: first/last name, email, international phone and explicit service/category selection before the scheduling calendar.
- Service category, platform, joining instructions and Cal.diy event type ID settings. Signed webhook records attendee phone and actual meeting details.
- Automatic client/organizer information emails, including pending versus confirmed status and real meeting joining details. Additive persistent outbox with SMTP retries, claims and per-reservation delivery status.
- Calendar invitations for all or individually selected confirmed bookings; independently choose client, organizer, both or neither. Single/bulk send and protected all/selected .ics downloads, bounded at 200.
- Stable calendar UID/sequence for reschedules and cancellation invitations for previously delivered addon invitations.
- No payments. Existing reservations are preserved by the additive booking-notifications migration.

Deployment: apply migrations, configure CMS SMTP, keep signed Cal.diy webhooks, require first/last name/email/attendeePhoneNumber on actual provider event types, connect actual conference providers and schedule /api/booking/notifications for retries. Calendar invitations require client acceptance; no silent OAuth account insertion is claimed. Choose one system for invitation delivery to avoid duplicates.
